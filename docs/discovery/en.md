**Niko is the warm, capable Discord companion for communities that care.** Moderation, music, progression, tickets, social feeds, and AI live in one polished bot, backed by a web dashboard that shows what is really happening in your server.

## Moderation that holds the line
Kick, ban, mute, tempmute, warn, purge, slowmode, and channel lockdown, with a case history you can look up later and moderation logs that keep your team on the same page. AutoMod catches spam, invite links, blocked words, mass mentions, join raids, and external-app abuse before a moderator has to step in.

## A community that comes back
- Giveaways with account-age, server-age, role, and booster requirements
- Persistent tickets with Discord-styled HTML transcripts
- Polls, suggestions, starboard, custom tags, birthdays, and reminders
- Social feed notifications for YouTube, Twitch, X, TikTok, Bluesky, and Reddit

## Progression worth chasing
Per-message XP with level curves, role rewards, and leaderboards, plus a full economy: jobs, banking, daily streaks, shops, collectibles, crime, robbery, and a weekly lottery. Achievements and standings render as server-side image cards, so every milestone looks the part.

## Music without the fuss
Lavalink-powered playback with queue, loop, shuffle, volume, and seek controls; YouTube, Spotify, and direct links; live now-playing cards, and Last.fm-powered autoplay when the queue runs dry.

## An AI companion that remembers
OpenAI-compatible chat with per-server and per-user memory, a configurable identity and tone, image generation, translation, and definitions — with settings that keep it inside the boundaries you set.

## One dashboard for everything
Sign in with Discord to manage settings, review activity and moderation insights, browse the live command directory, and read the documentation and changelog without leaving the dashboard.

## Speaks your server's language
Every command, panel, and help card ships in English, German, Spanish, and French, and follows the language each member picks.
