### Data removal policy

**Effective date:** 2026-10-20

This is the repository copy of the policy published at
https://niko.sryze.cc/data-removal. Keep it in sync with
`web/src/pages/DataRemovalPage.tsx`, the Privacy Policy, the Terms of Service,
and `/legal` in `src/cogs/legal/cog.py`.

**1. Who can request deletion**

Only the signed-in Discord account that owns the data can submit a request.
Server requests require the member to be the current owner of the server, or
to have been the owner at the moment Niko was removed. The server deletion
option therefore remains available after the bot has left, as long as the
ownership record exists.

**2. What account deletion removes**

Personal account data: economy balances and transactions, leveling XP, birthday
and reminder data, AFK state, music likes and playlists, giveaway entries, poll
votes, roleplay actions and blocks, AI conversation memory, voicemaster channels
owned by the member, and staff application submissions. The member's name is removed from
suggestions and giveaway host fields where it was attached to server content.
Gift attribution on premium subscriptions is cleared.

**3. What server deletion removes**

Niko's configuration and content for the server: AutoMod and logging settings,
AI settings and conversation memory, leveling configuration and XP, tickets
configuration, giveaways and entries,
polls and votes, starboard entries, social notifier follows, voicemaster
settings, prefix, profile, privacy, triggers, onboarding, suggestions, and the
server activity totals. Premium subscriptions and payment records are separate
financial records and are retained.

**4. What is always preserved**

Moderation and safety records are not deleted by an account or server request:

- bot-level user and server blacklists, and blacklist appeals;
- warnings, mutes, and moderation cases;
- moderation logs and ticket transcripts;
- giveaway bans;
- abuse strikes and safety investigations;
- scam-image reports and confirmed scam-image fingerprints.

Deleting everything else does not lift a restriction, restore access, or remove
evidence of abuse. Attempting to use a deletion request to bypass a safeguard
is a violation of the Terms of Service.

**5. Review and abuse prevention**

Every request is reviewed by the bot owner before deletion runs. One open
request is allowed per account or server, with a small daily limit. Pending
requests can be withdrawn. Requests are retained as a compliance audit record.
Deletion is irreversible; financial records that must be kept for accounting,
fraud prevention, refunds, or billing support are retained.

**6. Legal or court-ordered release**

If a law requires deletion or release of a preserved moderation or safety
record, do not use the public form. Send a formal request directly to the bot
owner through https://sorenthedev.indevs.in. It must include:

- a court record or a signed legal document;
- the contact details of an official legal representative who can verify the
  authenticity of that document.

These matters are handled personally and confidentially so private or
potentially identifying information stays private and legal disputes are
resolved quickly and professionally. Ordinary deletion requests never override
this process.

**7. Questions**

Open a private ticket in the support server: https://niko.sryze.cc/discord.
Include the request ID. Do not post identity documents in a public channel.
